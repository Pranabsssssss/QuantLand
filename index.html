Frequently Asked Questions

Got questions? We've got answers.

How quickly can I migrate my store data to QuantStock AI?
Under 5 minutes! Our AI Business Onboarding agent walks you through a quick 2-minute conversation or lets you drop your existing supplier CSV/Excel sheet. It automatically detects product names, SKUs, prices, and stock levels with zero manual rekeying.
Does QuantStock integrate with my existing POS like Square, Toast, or Clover?
Yes! QuantStock AI features 1-click bi-directional integrations with Square, Toast, Clover, Shopify POS, and Lightspeed. Sales made at your counter instantly deduct inventory in real time across all channels.
How does the Spoilage Sentinel prevent perishable food waste?
QuantStock tracks perishable batches using FIFO (First-In, First-Out) logic. When items like milk, berries, or baked goods near their expiration date, the system alerts your staff and can automatically prompt your POS to apply a limited-time discount to clear stock before it turns into loss.
Is my business data secure and confidential?
Your data security is guaranteed through industry-standard end-to-end encryption. All store metrics and inventory data remain completely private and secure.
